Research

Exploring stories, politics, and choices in interactive media

Lucas Sanchez is a doctoral student in Texts & Technology (Digital Media) at the University of Central Florida. His academic background brings together video game design and creative writing, with a focus on how interactive media can explore complex themes and engage players in meaningful ways.

Research Interests

Interactive Narrative

How can branching stories and character-driven design give players a meaningful role in shaping a game’s narrative?

Contemporary Topic Engagement

How can video games effectively address and explore current social, political, or cultural issues?

Computational & Immersive Media

What possibilities and challenges do emerging tools bring to game creation? Coursework has included generative AI, large language models, and virtual reality.
